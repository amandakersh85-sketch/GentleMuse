Thanksgiving

The hardest week of the run, and the week that decides whether the name means anything.

“I disagree with the origins of Thanksgiving because it's basically the violation and enslavement of indigenous people. I still like that Thanksgiving. I still have love and respect for thankfulness and gratitude and appreciativeness, that is part of my core existence.”

She does not have to choose, and the resolution is not a spin. Giving thanks at harvest was already here. The Wampanoag kept Thirteen Moons, thirteen thanksgivings a year on the lunar calendar: the first strawberries, the first green beans, the tapping of the maples, the storytelling month in deep winter. Millennia before anyone landed. Verified against the Smithsonian's Native Knowledge 360 and Plimoth Patuxet.

The gratitude is the oldest and truest part of the story, and it was already here. America took the practice, attached it to the wrong event, and spent 200 years building a myth around a harvest dinner nobody present thought was significant.
A trap, flagged before anyone posts it. “Thanksgiving was created to celebrate the Pequot massacre” is contested, and Snopes fact-checks it. True: Winthrop proclaimed a day of thanksgiving in 1637 after Mystic. Not supported: that this is the origin of the holiday. Days of thanksgiving were routine proclamations. Posting the causal version inside a series about details everyone repeats that are wrong would be self-refuting. The massacre can be covered on its own terms. The causal link cannot.
  • No lecturing, no guilt, no performance. Amanda is not apologising on anyone's behalf and is not telling her audience how to feel.
  • The gratitude is not a consolation prize bolted onto bad news. It is the finding.

Standing rules this campaign runs under

All set 10–11 Sept and written into AGENT-CONTRACT.md. None of these are up for re-litigation mid-campaign.

  • LinkedIn never gets holiday or seasonal. Business, AI, systems, lead magnets only.
  • 1 render per idea, reused on every platform. A second only for a genuine aspect-ratio change. Never 1 render per post.
  • Amanda being away is not a content problem. No blackout windows, no planning around her shifts or sleep or travel. The queue runs; she lives her life. The only thing that pauses is live human work, and a comment backlog is recoverable because public replies have no time limit.
  • A post carrying #ad or #TargetPartner never moves to another date. Same day is fine. Every bridge night is therefore date-locked, so the calendar has to be right the first time.
  • X stays off. 19 posts deleted 09/02, queue confirmed empty. Every X post carrying video failed on upload.
  • Every fact checked against live source, source named under each draft. No exceptions, no memory.
  • Graded 8+ before Amanda sees it. Hook leads with the turn. Numerals not spelled-out numbers, no em dashes, no hype, 4 to 5 hashtags.
  • Nothing goes into Blotato until Amanda reads and approves the copy.
