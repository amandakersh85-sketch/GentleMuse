# Cesa's channel: from dog honoring to conversion

**@cesasgoldenyears. Standard set 2026-08-26.** The channel had an engaged audience and no
way to convert it. Every comment was a lead that evaporated. That is fixed. This is how the
channel runs now.

## The offer is already built. Do not build another one.

The Cesa guide, "19 Years Old, 10 of Them Mine," IS the lead magnet for this audience. It has
a MailerLite group, a working 2-email delivery sequence, and it is enabled. Nothing new needs
making. It needed wiring, not inventing.

## How the wiring actually works, end to end

1. Someone comments CESA on any post on her channel.
2. Blotato automation 2952 fires and DMs them asking for their email address.
3. They reply with it. Blotato saves it to the contact.
4. The daily sync job adds that address to MailerLite group `196024300390581479` (Cesa).
5. Joining that group triggers "Cesa Guide Delivery," which sends "Her guide is here"
   immediately, then "Walk the house at her height" 4 days later.

Step 4 is the join that was missing. Without it the guide never sent, no matter how many
people asked. The sync now runs daily at 8:00 AM Central.

**The remaining latency:** up to 24 hours between someone giving their email and the guide
arriving. The DM copy was rewritten to say the guide is on its way to their inbox rather than
claiming it was already sent, because the old copy said "Sent." and that was not true.

**How to remove that lag entirely:** the form `196289170922014393` exists but was never
designed, so it is inactive and empty. A few minutes designing it in the MailerLite dashboard
gives it a working share URL. That URL then goes on the DM as a button and delivery becomes
instant self-serve, with the daily sync as backup rather than the main path.

## Every post on her channel carries a CTA now

2 keywords are live on the channel. Every new post uses 1 of them. No post goes up with no
ask, because that is what the channel was doing before.

| Keyword | Sends | Use it when the post is about |
|---|---|---|
| CESA | The Cesa guide | Her. Her age, her spot, her slowness, the small adjustments. |
| CONSIDER | Consider This newsletter | The home around her. Floors, layout, safety, the room. |

Roughly 2 CESA to 1 CONSIDER. CESA is the native ask on her channel and converts warmer.
CONSIDER earns its place because every Consider This issue closes with an "And for Cesa"
section, so it is not a bolt-on.

## Caption standard

1. **Hook grades 9 or it gets rebuilt.** Same bar as everything else. Specific, present tense,
   real tension. "She's 19 and she still finds the door when she hears my keys" over anything
   general about senior dogs.
2. **The observation carries the post.** What she actually did today, what it taught you about
   the room. Not dog facts. Not advice. The specific moment.
3. **1 keyword CTA, stated plainly.** "Comment CESA and I'll send you her guide."
4. **A share prompt where it fits.** "Send this to someone whose dog has slowed down."
   Shares are what carried the Press Play carousels to roughly 100 views.
5. Digits, no em dashes, no hype, 5 hashtags maximum.
6. Suggested tags: #seniordog #dogmom #chihuahua #cesasgoldenyears plus 1 situational.

## What is deliberately NOT on this channel

GUIDE and TUESDAY. Both serve the builder audience and would read as spam here. The channel
converts because the ask matches the reason people followed. Do not dilute it.
