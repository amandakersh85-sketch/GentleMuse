# Standing rule: the newsletters get promoted, every send day, without being asked

**Set by Amanda 2026-09-24. This does not expire and it is not a campaign.**

Her instruction, on the day the Consider This runway warning fired: we need to keep promoting
the newsletters. Writing the issues is not the work. An issue that sends to a list nobody was
invited to join is the same as not sending it.

**Every real conversion this account has ever had came through a landing page, not a keyword.**
As of 2026-09-24 no audience member has ever used a keyword. That is exactly why the promo
posts matter: the post is what puts the landing page in front of somebody. The keyword CTA
rides along, it does not carry the load.

## The rule

**SUPERSEDED AND WIDENED 2026-09-24 by Amanda, by voice.** Send days alone were too thin. The
rule is now a standing rotation: **a newsletter promo goes out at least every other day,
carousels only, recycling the sets that already exist.** The cycle, the asset inventory and the
2 open blockers are in **[`NEWSLETTER-ROTATION.md`](NEWSLETTER-ROTATION.md)**. Read that first.

What is below still holds underneath it. Send days are the floor, not the plan.

**No Consider This or Just Another Tuesday send day goes out with an empty queue on the
channels that promote it.** Whoever holds the posting project fills those days as part of
ordinary refill, the same as any other slot. Nobody needs to ask for it first.

This already went wrong once. On 2026-09-09, 6 of the 7 Just Another Tuesday send days had no
posts scheduled at all, so the newsletter went unpromoted on the exact mornings it sent. The
drafts written to fix that are in `../DRAFT_0909_jat-send-day-promo-posts.txt`.

## What a send day post has to carry

- **A carousel, never a single image.** Single-image newsletter promos are retired as of
  2026-09-24. Do not schedule another one.
- The specific hook, never the word "newsletter." People sign up for the towels, the sponge,
  the pillow. Nobody signs up for a newsletter. This is finding 3 in `../STRATEGY.md` and it
  has not changed.
- The verified link for that offer, from the table in `AGENT-CONTRACT.md`. Consider This is
  `https://consider-this.subscribepage.io`. Just Another Tuesday is
  `https://just-another-tuesday-gm.subscribepage.io`. No `preview.mailerlite.io` links, no
  `gentlemuse.co/reset-guide`.
- The keyword CTA where the channel uses one. CONSIDER and SEASONAL on Cesa's account,
  TUESDAY on Amanda's. Ride the existing automations, do not create new ones.
- The ladder times in `../SCHEDULING-LADDER.md`. Instagram @thegentlemuse2026 owns 10:00 AM
  Central and the send-day post does not get to jump that queue.

## What this rule does not authorize

- **It does not authorize scheduling.** `claude/holiday-caption-strategy-m5abq8` is lead on the
  posting project as of 2026-09-02 by Amanda's decision. Other sessions draft and leave the
  draft here.
- **It does not authorize LinkedIn seasonal posts.** LinkedIn gets business posts only. A
  newsletter promo qualifies when it is about what she learned, not when it is a holiday hook.
- **It does not revive X.** X is off.
- **It does not change the 200 post cap or the render reuse rule.** A send-day promo reuses an
  existing render wherever one exists for that idea.
- **Nothing sends without Amanda's approval.** Drafts wait for her, the same as the issues do.
