**From:** Claude, on Amanda's instruction, 2026-09-10
**Deliverable:** 4 background plates, 2 sizes each, 8 files total
**Why:** Amanda's HeyGen avatar "Amanda" needs backgrounds that are not the hotel room she

## The rule this kit exists under

`AGENT-CONTRACT.md` section 0-RATIO: **1 render per idea, reused everywhere.**

A background plate is the purest case of that rule. **4 plates, made once, used in every video
Amanda ever makes.** Not 1 per video. Not 1 per post. If a plate is good, it is good for a year.
Spend the effort here accordingly, this is the highest-leverage image set in the whole account.

---

## Hard requirements, all 4 plates

- **NO PEOPLE.** Not in focus, not in the background, not reflected, not a photo of a person on
  a shelf. The avatar is the only human in the frame.
- **NO READABLE TEXT.** Book spines, signage, screens, labels: all illegible. HeyGen composites
  the avatar over this and mangled lettering behind her head is the fastest way to look cheap.
- **Empty centre.** The avatar stands centre frame, roughly the middle 40% horizontally, from
  about 25% down to the bottom edge. Everything interesting lives in the outer thirds. A plate
  with its best detail dead centre is a wasted plate.
- **Shallow depth of field.** Background softly out of focus, as if shot at f/2. The plate must
  read as *behind* her, not as a flat wallpaper she is pasted onto.
- **Contrast at the edges.** Amanda's avatar has dark hair and wears black. Where her head and
  shoulders will sit, the plate must be LIGHTER than dark hair. A dark plate behind dark hair
  produces a halo and kills the illusion.
- **Warm daylight**, soft, from one side. No hard shadows, no coloured studio gels.
- Photographic realism. Not illustration, not 3D render.

## Sizes, each plate
- `1080 x 1920` (9:16) — TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Primary.
- `1080 x 1350` (4:5) — Instagram feed and Facebook. Recompose, do not stretch or crop-and-pray.

## The 4 plates

### 01 · `study` — the one she asked for
Rebuild the room from her Day 58 video. A warm home study seen from a seated desk position.
Left third: a black metal open shelving unit, warm white fairy lights woven through it, book
spines laid flat and upright in cream, rust and deep red, all illegible. A coral neon sign
glowing on the upper left shelf, its shape suggestive but its letters unreadable. A small brass
object on a lower shelf. Right third: a tall floor lamp with a warm linen shade, lit, and a
darker wooden shelf behind it. Warm neutral wall between them. Foreground bottom edge: the front
lip of a dark walnut desk, in focus, running the width of the frame.
**Centre must be the plain warm wall.** Use for: business, AI, Just Another Tuesday, anything
where she is talking about building this.

### 02 · `kitchen` — practical and household
A kitchen corner in warm morning light. Left: the edge of pale cabinetry and a butcher block
counter with a stoneware crock holding wooden spoons. Right: a window out of focus, bright but
not blown out, a trailing plant on the sill. Centre: plain warm wall and upper cabinet, clean.
Nothing branded, no product labels. Use for: Consider This, anything about the house.

### 03 · `sitting-room` — soft and personal
A living room in late afternoon. Left: the arm of a linen sofa and a low table with a stack of
books and a cold mug. Right: a floor lamp and the corner of a curtained window, warm light
coming through. A folded throw blanket. Centre: warm wall, uninterrupted.
Use for: Cesa, personal, anything gentle. No dog in the plate, Cesa is filmed, not generated.

### 04 · `plain` — the workhorse
No furniture, no props. A warm neutral wall in soft daylight with a gentle vertical falloff,
slightly brighter behind where her head sits, softly darker at the outer edges. A barely visible
plaster texture so it does not read as a flat colour fill.
Use for: anything where the message is the point and the room is a distraction. This will end up
being the most used plate in the kit. Make it genuinely good, not an afterthought.
