# Welcome Email — Consider This

**Why this exists:** Right now, when someone signs up for the newsletter, they get the
double opt-in confirmation and then nothing until the next scheduled Wednesday issue.
A welcome email closes that gap: it confirms they made a good choice, sets the
expectation, and asks for a reply (which helps deliverability and gives you content ideas).

**Trigger:** Subscriber joins the "Consider This" group.
**Status:** Draft. Install only on Amanda's approval.

---

**Subject:** You're in. Here's what Consider This is.

**Preview text:** One email a week. One overlooked thing in your home.

**Body:**

Hi, and thanks for signing up.

Consider This is one short email a week. I pick one thing in your home you've probably stopped noticing, tell you what's actually going on with it, and whether it's worth 2 minutes of your time. That's the whole thing. No 40-step routines. No pressure to buy anything.

Your first issue lands soon. Recent ones have covered the washer gasket that never gets clean, why microwaving a sponge doesn't sanitize it, and the furnace filter that's rated highest but wrong for most houses.

One thing to do now, if you want: hit reply and tell me the one spot in your home that's been bugging you. I read every reply, and it helps me pick what to write about.

Talk soon,
Amanda
The Gentle Muse

*You can unsubscribe any time using the link below. No hard feelings.*

---

## Optional second email (24 hours later) — send the best issue

Send your strongest existing issue as the "welcome gift" so they get value before the
weekly cadence starts. Suggested: "The part of your washer that never gets clean" or
"Microwaving your sponge isn't sanitizing it." Both are already written.
