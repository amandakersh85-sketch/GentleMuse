She is right. **The queue posts without her. That is what it is for.** Nov 5 to 11 is not a gap,
is not a hazard, and is not a week to plan around. It is 7 ordinary nights that happen to
publish while she is somewhere better. She has been scheduling weeks ahead all along; the queue
already runs to Oct 31.

**Never again frame her being away as a content problem.** The correct posture: the machine runs,
she lives her life, and the only thing that changes is the pace of things that genuinely need a

### What genuinely needs her, and therefore has a real deadline

Exactly 1 thing: **approval.** She reads and approves copy before it goes into Blotato. Nobody
else can do that step.

So the November batch goes to her **before Nov 5**, not because the cruise is dangerous, but
because that is when she is easiest to reach. Working back: drafted and graded by **Oct 25**,
approved by **Oct 31**, scheduled by **Nov 4**. December has until roughly Nov 20.

That is a courtesy deadline, not a risk mitigation. If she approves from the ship, fine.

### What actually pauses

Live human work, and only that: comment replies and anything needing her judgment in the moment.
Automations keep firing and keep delivering. **A DM backlog is recoverable** because public
comment replies have no time limit and do not consume the single private-reply slot. So a queue
of comments waiting on Nov 12 is normal, not damage.

## Decisions, settled

### 1. Religious content: everything, including the hard stuff
Her call, made with the risk stated. The December 25 dating question, what the earliest sources
do and do not say, how midwinter festivals were absorbed rather than replaced, the Puritan bans
in their own words.

**The risk she accepted, restated so nobody softens it later:** her audience skews women 40+ in
the Midwest. Some will read careful history as an attack on their faith. She weighed that and
said go.

**How it gets handled:** curiosity, never correction. The tone is "here is what the record says,"
never "here is what you got wrong." No post tells anyone what to believe. Every one of these is
checked against source and the source is named, same as the 32 plates.

### 2. Commerce: separate lanes, bridges only where they genuinely earn it
She first picked "weave throughout" and then described something different in her own words.
**Her words win.** What she actually said: *"it genuinely has to serve a deep and meaningful
purpose for us to connect an affiliate link to it"* and *"we don't want to over commercialize it
... somebody follows me because they enjoy watching those facts series."*

So: **history is the default. Affiliate appears only when the fact leads there honestly.**

Her own worked example, kept because it is the standard: a fact about turnips, gourds and
pumpkins, into how modern decoration actually started, and only then the glass pumpkin. Fact
first, segue that stands on its own, product last. **If the bridge feels forced it does not get
built.**

When a bridge is built it carries the full kit, no exceptions: `#ad`, `#TargetPartner`, the
disclosure line, and a **direct item link**, never a bare storefront. On TikTok,
`isBrandedContent: true`.

**Consequence she has been told:** her standing rule is that a post carrying `#ad` or
`#TargetPartner` never moves to another date. Every bridge night is therefore date-locked. Get
the calendar right the first time.
