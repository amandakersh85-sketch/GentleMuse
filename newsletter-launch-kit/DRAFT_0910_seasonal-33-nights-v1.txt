Trick or treat started as a transaction, and the payment was prayer.

Medieval souling: the poor went door to door on All Souls' Day asking for small spiced cakes.
For every soul cake you took, you owed a prayer for that household's dead.

The candy is the part that survived. The debt is the part that got dropped.

Somebody has been keeping score on your behalf for 800 years and you were never told.

NIGHT 2 — 2026-09-23
================================================================================
Bobbing for apples was not a game. It was a way of finding out who you would marry.

Romans brought Pomona, goddess of orchards, to the British Isles, and her apple landed on top
of Celtic Samhain divination. Unmarried people bit at floating apples. First to bite was next to
marry. Sometimes names were cut into the skin first.

We kept the tub and threw out the question.

Most traditions are old questions wearing a party hat.

NIGHT 3 — 2026-09-27
================================================================================
Iowa kids have had to earn the candy since 1938, and most of the country has no idea.

Des Moines police logged more than 550 vandalism reports on a single Halloween night in 1938.
Fires. Windows put through with bricks. So the city moved the whole thing to October 30, called
it Beggars' Night, and told children they had to tell a joke at the door to get anything.

It worked. The vandalism dropped and the joke stuck for 86 years.

A rule invented to stop arson turned into the part everybody local remembers fondest.

NIGHT 4 — 2026-10-01
================================================================================
Trick or treating was crowd control.

By 1933 Halloween pranks had stopped being funny. Cars flipped, telephone poles cut down, more
than $100,000 in property damage a year in big cities. Several were seriously discussing banning
Halloween outright.

So towns built the alternative: costumes, a route, parents watching, candy at the end. Give the
mischief a shape and it stops being mischief.

That is the whole trick. You rarely stop a behavior. You give it somewhere to go.

NIGHT 5 — 2026-10-02
================================================================================
The most popular art of the plague years put the pope and the peasant in the same line.

The Danse Macabre. Skeletons leading the living to the grave in a procession, and the point was
the order of the line: emperor, pope, merchant, child, farmhand. The first known painted one went
up around 1424 on the wall of a Paris charnel house.

It was not made to frighten anyone. It was made to level them.

Nothing in your century has been that honest on a public wall.

The beaked plague mask is not medieval. It shows up 300 years after the Black Death.

Charles de Lorme, a French court physician, is credited with the design around 1619. The beak
was packed with lavender, mint, myrrh, vinegar sponges, because the theory was that plague
traveled in bad smelling air.

It did nothing. The Black Death had already come and gone 3 centuries earlier.

The costume everyone wears to mean "the plague" was invented for the wrong plague, and it did not
work on that one either.

NIGHT 7 — 2026-10-05
================================================================================
In 1892 a Rhode Island town dug up a 19 year old girl and burned her heart.

Mercy Brown. Tuberculosis had taken her mother, her sister, and was taking her brother Edwin.
The village decided 1 of the dead was feeding on the living. They exhumed 3 bodies on March 17.
The other 2 had decomposed normally. Mercy, buried 2 months in winter ground, had not.

They burned her heart and liver, mixed the ashes into water, and gave it to Edwin to drink.

He died 2 months later. She was the last person exhumed as a vampire in New England.

That was 1892. Electric light existed. Grief just does not care what year it is.

Bram Stoker never went to Transylvania. He got it from a library.

He wrote Dracula from books, mostly in the London Library and at Whitby, where he spent 4 weeks

That footnote is the whole connection. There is no evidence he ever knew who Vlad the Impaler
was.

The most famous vampire in the world is a research project by a man who never left Britain.

A court ordered every copy of Nosferatu destroyed, and the film is still here.

Prana-Film made it in 1922 without buying the rights to Dracula. Bram Stoker's widow Florence

was public domain, and by 1929 it was playing in US theaters.

You cannot recall a thing once enough copies of it exist. That was true on film stock and it is
more true now.

NIGHT 16 — 2026-10-14

The Ouija board was a business before it was ever spooky.

Charles Kennard formed the Kennard Novelty Company in Baltimore in 1890 with investors, one of
them Elijah Bond. Patent 446,054 issued February 10, 1891. To get it, Bond had to demonstrate the
thing to the chief patent officer and spell out his name.

By 1892 they had a second Baltimore factory, 2 in New York, 2 in Chicago and 1 in London.

Even the name was marketing. They asked the board what to call it and printed the answer.

The most haunted object in your grandmother's closet is a toy with a patent number on it.

NIGHT 23 — 2026-10-21
================================================================================
Night of the Living Dead lost its copyright over a title change.

It was called Night of the Flesh Eaters. The distributor changed the title, made new prints, and
forgot to carry over the copyright notice. Under the 1909 Copyright Act, no notice meant no
copyright, permanently.

The film fell into the public domain in 1968 and stayed there. George Romero tried to sue. The
distributor went bankrupt in the 70s and dissolved.

Anyone could use it. So everyone did, and that is a large part of why the modern zombie exists at
all.

The most expensive mistake in horror history is also the reason the genre spread.

There is no full moon this Halloween, and there will not be one until 2039.

Between 1900 and 2100 the moon is full on October 31 exactly 12 times. The last one visible in
every time zone on earth was 2020. Before that, 1944.

It runs on the Metonic cycle, roughly 19 years. And every Halloween full moon is automatically a
blue moon, because the lunar cycle is 29.5 days, so a full moon on the 31st means there was
already one on the 1st or 2nd.

Every drawing of Halloween has a full moon in it. The actual sky almost never cooperates.

Día de los Muertos is not Mexican Halloween. It is not even on Halloween.

November 1 and 2. It runs from Aztec practice honoring Mictecacihuatl, and it originally took

which is the only reason it sits next to Halloween on the calendar at all.

November 1 is for the children. November 2 is for the adults.

Different holiday, different dead, different job. Sharing a week is not the same as sharing a
