## The 3 findings that are costing you sign-ups

### 1. Your newsletter reaches 1 person, not 9 (fix this first)
The "Consider This" campaigns are filtered to the "Consider This" group, which has
**1 active subscriber**. Your other 8 subscribers came in through the guides and quiz,
so they sit in different groups and are excluded. Tomorrow's issue goes to 1 inbox.

**Options (your call, because it affects who gets emailed):**
- **Recommended:** Send a one-time, friendly "I started a weekly thing, want in?" email
  to your existing 8 subscribers with the signup link, so they opt in by choice.
- Or: add existing active subscribers to the Consider This group directly (faster, but
  they didn't specifically ask for the newsletter, so the invite is cleaner).
- Going forward: add a step to each lead-magnet automation that invites new subscribers
  into the newsletter.

### 2. Signing up lands people in silence
New newsletter sign-ups get the double opt-in confirmation, then nothing until the next
Wednesday. No welcome, no warmth. Draft welcome email is in `welcome-email.md`.
**Recommended:** install it as a "joins Consider This group" automation.

### 3. "Sign up for my newsletter" is a weak offer (0 sign-ups from 5 form opens)
People don't sign up for newsletters. They sign up for a *specific* thing. Two fixes:
- **Content:** every launch post leads with a specific hook (the towels, the sponge,
  the list), not the word "newsletter." Drafts are in `launch-content.md`.
- **Offer:** point cold traffic at a free lead magnet (Reset Guide / AI Beginner's
  Guide). It converts better and still grows the list. Then invite those people into
  the newsletter (see finding 1).

Optional lever: the form uses double opt-in (people must confirm in email). Good for
deliverability, adds friction. Worth watching; not changing without your say-so.
