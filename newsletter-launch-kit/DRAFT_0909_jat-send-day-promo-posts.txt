POST 5 — Tuesday 10/20, issue #009, Day 14 financial audit
GRADE 8.5/10 (hook 4, body 4.5)
=====================================================================

I chased down a $9.23 line item this summer. I could have rounded it and nobody would ever
have known.

The rule I set was that I was not allowed to leave an unknown as an unknown. So: $350 in cash
was the air conditioning and the brakes. $2,400 in transfers was me moving money into my own 2
businesses. And $9.23 was interest.

What that hour actually produced was not a tidy ledger. It was finding out the month ran a
deficit, and why. A semi annual insurance premium had landed, and the water bill had come in
at roughly 3 times normal.

Neither is a spending problem. Both are a timing problem, and a monthly total is exactly the
shape that hides one.

If I had rounded the $9.23 I would have rounded the water bill too.
