## 2. Opener doctrine (applies to every cut from here on)

No fade-from-black. First frame = thumbnail = hook. Open on: motion already
happening, a face mid-expression, a product in hand, or text already on
screen. When Amanda films, she front-loads a visual hook; when cutting, scan
the first 3 seconds of every raw clip and start the cut at the strongest
frame, not at file start.

## 3. Text-on-screen b-roll series (the 7-second/10-second-read trick)

Short b-roll, designed text on screen (not subtitles), text takes longer to
read than the clip runs, so it loops. 8 concepts, each mapped to footage:

1. **Coffee pour + Cesa waiting** (new footage, she films Sat AM)
   Text: "She waits for the coffee to pour before she starts her day. 19
   years old and still better at mornings than me."
2. **Cesa slow walk across kitchen** (existing camera roll)
   Text: "She's 19. She has never once hurried. She's also the only one in
   this house who always ends up exactly where she needs to be."
3. **Sink emptying, hands in frame** (film Sunday during real reset)
   Text: "My whole reset: empty the sink, write 3 lines for Monday, do all 4
   skincare steps. 20 minutes. That's the secret. There's no secret."
4. **Kitchen table, laptop open, evening light** (film Sunday night)
   Text: "Full shift today. Now the kitchen table shift. 66 followers and
   building anyway. This is the part nobody films."
5. **Walk outside, sidewalk POV** (existing or new)
   Text: "Nothing that helped this week cost money. Sunlight. A walk with an
   old dog. A clean counter. A 3 line list."
6. **Target aisle POV** (tomorrow's raw Target footage)
   Text: "I walked in for 1 thing. The heyday wall had other plans." Caption
   carries the product SKU link, per the hard rule.
7. **Planner flat lay, pen moving** (existing camera roll)
   Text: "Every paycheck gets a job before it gets spent. Bills. Food. Gas.
   Savings. Peace. In that order."
8. **Laundry on the bed, folding** (existing camera roll)
   Text: "You don't need a new life. You need 20 quiet minutes and a shorter
   list. Comment RESET and I'll send you mine."
