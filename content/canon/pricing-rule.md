# The pricing rule, correctly scoped

Corrected by Amanda 13 Sep 2026 after it was misapplied several times.

## What the rule actually is

**She cannot state prices for TikTok Shop items she links on TikTok.** That is the
entire rule. It comes from a TikTok Shop violation on 4 Aug 2026 over a price
mismatch, and it is enforced by TikTok Shop, against TikTok Shop listings.

## What the rule is NOT

- **It is not a Target rule.** Club Target has no pricing penalty. Target never
  issued one and none exists.
- **It does not apply to Club Target posts.** Those carry `club.target.com`
  affiliate links, not TikTok Shop listings. A price in a Club Target caption is
  not a violation of anything.
- **It does not apply to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X, LinkedIn or Pinterest.**
- **It does not apply to her own life.** What she buys, what was on clearance, what
  she paid, said in conversation or in content that links nothing.

## Scope in one line

    TikTok Shop items + linked on TikTok = no price.
    Everything else = no restriction.

## What this got wrong

On 7 Sep 2026 price language was stripped from 3 scheduled Club Target TikTok
captions and 1 published one was flagged for her to edit, on the reasoning that the
TikTok Shop violation applied. **It did not.** Those were `club.target.com` affiliate
posts, not TikTok Shop listings. The edits were unnecessary. They did no harm, the
captions still read well, but the justification was wrong and it was presented to her
as urgent under a deadline.

It was then repeated on 12 Sep as a blanket "no prices in any frame" filming rule,
and again on 13 Sep about a bottle of wine she had not even filmed.

## The correction

Do not apply this rule outside TikTok Shop. Do not describe it as a Target rule. Do
not raise it about things she buys. If a Club Target caption names a price, that is
fine.
