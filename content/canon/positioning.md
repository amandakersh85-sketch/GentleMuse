## What this settles

**The food reviews are not off-niche.** She raised the worry herself: "I know it's
not in my niche, quote unquote, but it is, actually." She is right. The niche is
not a topic, it is her. An accountability coach who reviews local food and works
retail full time is one coherent person. A feed that is only one of those three
reads as a brand account, not a person.

**Her stated reason for the rotation:** "I like for my food reviews to get cycled
through, so that way people are used to seeing me as a food critic and food
reviewer... I don't want people to just think I'm one note."

So the food reviews are a deliberate positioning play, not filler. **Treat them as
a standing content pillar, not as something to fit in around Club Target.**

**Vending and laundromat are phase 2.** Do not pitch them, plan them, or fold them
into content. She named them so they are on the record, and then said not yet.
