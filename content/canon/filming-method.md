# How Amanda films. Current as of 15 Sep 2026.

**This file exists because the rule changed and stale handoffs were about to cause
a session to reject her own good footage.**

## The state of it

On **12 Sep** Amanda said, in her own words: "Okay, not going to do a fitting room
thing. Uh, just not going to happen. In addition, I will take some pictures of SKUs
and tags or whatever, but we have to understand, um, I am not doing an on-camera
thing. It's going to be strictly me filming footage."

That was written into every handoff as a hard rule: never on camera, no fitting
room, no try-on, silent b-roll only.

On **15 Sep** she published `4448610`, a Club Target Instagram post whose video
contains a fitting room mirror selfie, an on-screen card reading "THE FITTING
ROOM", and a closing shot of her wearing the ribbed knit dress and olive cardigan.
The caption says "it all came together in the fitting room."

So she did the fitting room thing. That is entirely her call and it is her own
footage of herself.

## What this means for anyone producing for her

**Do not treat "never on camera" as a hard constraint any more, and do not treat
"always on camera" as one either.** Both readings are wrong now.

- Her published Club Target work through 12 Sep is **talking head**, on camera,
  speaking.
- The 14 Sep denim TikTok is **silent b-roll**, no face.
- The 15 Sep fall fits post is **on camera, in a fitting room**.

She moves between all three. **Cut what the footage actually contains.** Look at
the frames before you write a caption, and never write one that claims a shot the
footage does not have.

**If you are planning a shoot rather than cutting existing footage, ask her.** Do
not assume she will be on camera and do not assume she will not.

## What has not changed

- **Never generate a synthetic voice.** If she supplies a voice memo, drop it into
  the render natively. Otherwise build the edit to work silent.
- **Product and aisle footage is always safe.** Shelf, rack, endcap, item picked up
  and turned, item in the cart.
- **On-screen `#TargetPartner` in the opening frames** of every Club Target video.
  See `content/canon/disclosure-rule.md`. All three recent videos do this correctly.
