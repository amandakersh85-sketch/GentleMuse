DAYLIGHT SAVING, THE THING THAT MAKES THIS WAVE DIFFERENT
Central drops to CST on Sunday 1 November 2026, and this wave straddles it. Slots
are written in UTC but chosen to hold LOCAL time constant, so the times change
mid-file on purpose:

  before 1 Nov (CDT, UTC-5)   instagram 15:00 and 23:00, tiktok 15:00,
                              facebook 17:10, youtube 17:20, x and linkedin 13:30
  from 1 Nov (CST, UTC-6)     instagram 16:00 and 23:00, tiktok 16:00,
                              facebook 18:10, youtube 18:20, x and linkedin 14:30

A fixed 15:00 UTC slot would quietly become 9 AM Central on 1 November, an hour off
the peak, and nothing would have looked wrong in the file. scripts/validate-wave.py
now checks this per row and fails the file if a slot drifts.
