  third. No category label. "Random trivia fact of the day" never appears on
  screen or in the audio again.

  of the hook punching in under the first.
- All text sits in the upper or lower third, off the avatar's face and torso.
- Render clean from HeyGen. Upload that file directly to each platform.
  Never re-download from TikTok. Flip Instagram's own AI toggle.

  no hype. Instagram max 5 hashtags, Facebook none.

## Verification status, read this before approving

- **[V]** = verified with a source this session, or so well established it is
  not in dispute.
- **[C]** = high confidence from established knowledge, but the specific
  number in it gets a source check on the day it renders.

## Week 1 — home and cleaning lead

**Day 1, Tue Sept 8. HOME. [V] SHIPPED**
Dryer sheets leave a film on the lint screen that cuts airflow 25 to 50
percent. Test: run water over it, if it pools instead of pouring through it is
coated. Warm water and dish soap.
Frame 1: **IT LOOKS CLEAN.** → **IT ISN'T.**

**Day 2, Wed Sept 9. BODY. [V]**
You cannot tickle yourself. Your cerebellum predicts your own movement and
cancels the sensation before you feel it.
Frame 1: **TRY TO TICKLE YOURSELF.** → **YOU CAN'T.**

**Day 3, Thu Sept 10. MONEY. [V]**
Checking your own credit is a soft pull. It does not lower your score. Only
lender hard pulls do that, and people avoid checking for a reason that is not
real.
Frame 1: **CHECKING YOUR CREDIT** → **DOESN'T HURT IT.**

**Day 4, Fri Sept 11. HOME. [V]**
Most modern dishwashers have a removable filter in the floor of the tub. It
needs cleaning by hand. Many people have owned one for years and never taken
it out.
Frame 1: **YOUR DISHWASHER HAS A FILTER.** → **WHEN DID YOU LAST CLEAN IT?**

**Day 5, Sat Sept 12. BODY. [C]**
You are measurably taller in the morning. Spinal discs compress through the
day and rebound overnight.
Frame 1: **YOU SHRINK EVERY DAY.** → **AND GROW BACK OVERNIGHT.**

**Day 6, Sun Sept 13. HOME. [V] SAFETY**
Never mix bleach with ammonia. It makes chloramine gas. Plenty of glass
cleaners contain ammonia, which is how this happens by accident.
Frame 1: **BLEACH PLUS GLASS CLEANER** → **MAKES TOXIC GAS.**

**Day 7, Mon Sept 14. MONEY. [V]**

Frame 1: **YOUR STATE MAY OWE YOU MONEY.** → **SEARCHING IS FREE.**
