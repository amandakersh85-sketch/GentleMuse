# STORE RUN: Target Beauty Studio

One challenge, one section of the store, roughly 15 minutes of filming.

**Challenge:** Meet Target Beauty Studio, `club.target.com/t/0pp2`
**Brief, Target's words:** "Explore Target's new beauty destination and share your
faves in a haul or first-look review."
**Dropped** 14 Sep 2026. Instagram plus TikTok, Tier 3+.
**Hashtag: UNKNOWN.** Grab it off the card before posting. A wrong tag scores 0.
**Closing date: UNKNOWN.** Grab that too.

Target announced Beauty Studio on 26 Aug as its "destination for what's new, now
and next in beauty", so it is a remodeled section, not a product line. Find the
section, not an item.

## THE SHOT LIST. Film in this order.

Everything is product and shelf footage. None of it requires her on camera, and
none of it requires her to talk. If she wants to be in it, that works too, but
build it so it stands up silent.

1. **The entrance to the section.** Wide, one slow walk in. Whatever signage says
   Beauty Studio. This is the opening frame and it has to establish that this is a
   new place, so hold it longer than feels natural, about 4 seconds.
2. **One slow pan down the main aisle.** Steady, waist height, no jerking.
3. **The testers or the display bar,** if there is one. New sections usually have a
   try-it counter. That is the single most "this is new" shot available.
4. **The K-beauty shelf.** 3 to 4 seconds.
5. **Three products picked up and turned.** Pick ones she actually likes. Front of
   package, then turn to show the side. 3 seconds each. These are the "faves" the
   brief asks for.
6. **Anything with a NEW or ONLY AT TARGET flag on the shelf edge.** Those flags do
   the storytelling for her.
7. **The end of the aisle, walking out.** Gives the edit somewhere to land.

## GRAB WHILE SHE IS STANDING THERE

**The medicube Kojic Acid Turmeric Jelly Gel Mask shelf tag.** Photograph the tag
so the SKU number is readable and in focus.

This is not for the challenge. Automation `453` FB MASK has been broken since
8 Aug, pointing at the bare storefront instead of the product, because nobody has
had that SKU. medicube is K-beauty, so it should be in or near this section. It is
the last outstanding item from the 12 Sep run.

Also photograph the shelf tag of anything from shot 5, so the post can link the
actual product page rather than the storefront.

## WHAT NOT TO BOTHER WITH

- **No fitting room, no mirror, nothing that needs her to change.** This is a
  beauty section, that is not the ask.
- **No swatching on camera unless she wants to.** The brief says haul or first look.
  A first look at the section satisfies it entirely.
- **Do not buy anything to make this work.** A first-look review needs no purchase.
  If she does buy, the same footage also feeds Share a Target Find and Post your
  Target Haul.

## RULES FOR THE EDIT

- **`#TargetPartner` burned into the opening frames**, near the product, and
  repeated around the midpoint. End card alone does not count and is the likely
  reason her board read 0%. See `content/canon/disclosure-rule.md`.
- `#TargetPartner` first in the caption, inside the first 2 lines, plus `#ClubTarget`
  and the real challenge hashtag once she has it.
- Disclosure line: "I'm a Target partner, so I may earn rewards or commission when
  you shop my link, at no extra cost to you."
- **Prices are fine.** Shelf tags in frame are fine. The price rule is TikTok Shop
  only and does not touch Club Target. See `content/canon/pricing-rule.md`.
- TikTok is the only placement that pays until she passes 500 followers on
  Instagram. Cut TikTok first.

As of 16 Sep, Beauty Studio is **the only open challenge with no footage at all.**

Everything else is covered: denim, wellness reset, fall fits, game day, halloween
decor and play picks are published or queued through 20 Sep off the 12 Sep run.
The queue then goes empty, so this run is also what keeps Club Target content
