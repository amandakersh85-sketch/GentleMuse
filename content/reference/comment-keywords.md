## Notes

- **SEASONAL and CONSIDER both deliver Consider This.** Two keywords, one
  destination. Intentional as separate entry points (seasonal facts vs home
  maintenance), but worth knowing they land in the same place.
- SEASONAL is the most used keyword in the corpus, then CESA. Those 2 carry the
  program.
- **RESET and TUESDAY are barely used**, 2 uses each, and both have finished
  products behind them. Underused inventory.
- DISHWASHER was a one off product DM, not a lead magnet. Do not treat it as a
  standing keyword.
