## The math, stated honestly

Needed: +810 in 30 days, or about 27 net follows per day.

Reference points from real data:
- Non-campaign baseline: 134 to 190 over roughly 2 weeks, about 4 per day.
- Peak campaign rate: +18 per day, sustained for 4 days in August.

27 per day is roughly 6 times the current baseline and 1.5 times the best day
ever recorded, sustained for 30 days instead of 4. This is a stretch goal and
should be treated as one. Realistic bands:

- Baseline continues: about 310 by Oct 8
- August campaign rate sustained: about 730
- Campaign rate plus 1 breakout Cesa reel: 1,000 is reachable
- The number that makes it work is a Cesa post above 10,000 views

The plan below is built to maximize the odds of that breakout rather than to
grind 27 per day, because grinding 27 per day is not achievable with the
current follower base. Publicly the counter stays honest either way. Missing
1,000 and reporting the real number is on brand and cost nothing last time.

## Trivia fix, diagnosed from actual frames

Frames pulled from the live Instagram trivia reel on 09/08. Three problems,
in order of severity:

1. **A "TikTok, AI Cast" badge is burned into the top right corner from frame
   1.** Amanda confirms the source is HeyGen with her avatar, which means the
   Instagram copy was downloaded from TikTok after posting rather than
   uploaded from the clean HeyGen render. Instagram demotes both AI labels and
   competitor platform watermarks. This is the primary cause of 37 views
   against 230 to 577 on Facebook.
   **Fix: upload the clean HeyGen file directly to every platform. Never
   re-download from TikTok. This is now a standing rule.**

2. **Frame 1 is a category header, not a hook.** It opens on "RANDOM TRIVIA
   FACT OF THE DAY" in the top third. That is a label, which the opener
   doctrine bans outright.
   **Fix: frame 1 carries the fact's most surprising 3 to 5 words, large,
   already on screen. No category label anywhere in the first 2 seconds.
   The words "random trivia fact of the day" never appear on screen again.**

3. **Static for all 12 seconds.** Gray blazer, gray background, presenter
   centered, text stacked over her torso, only a pulsing brain graphic moves.
   **Fix: a visual beat by second 2. A punch in on the avatar, a hard cut to
   a full frame graphic, or the fact text scaling on the key word. Something
   has to change size or position inside the first 2 seconds.**

Also: move the caption text off the presenter's torso. It currently competes
with her face, which is the one thing that holds attention in a talking frame.

### Trivia test protocol

Run 5 clean exports over 5 days on Instagram. Compare against the 37 view
baseline and the Facebook 230 to 577 band.
- If Instagram lands above 150, the badge was the whole problem, keep going.
- If it lands 37 to 150, the format is weak on Instagram regardless. Keep it
  on Facebook and TikTok, drop the Instagram version.
- Facebook keeps running unchanged during the test. It works there.
