## 3. Red team, with the data Avery must supply

Four attacks were raised. Each one resolves into a question only Avery can
answer, because answering it requires the real balances and real rate quotes.

### 3.1 The spread may not justify the lock  [HIGHEST PRIORITY]
As of August 2026, CDs broadly run about 4.00 to 5.00 percent APY, and business
savings products are competitive with that and sometimes higher. If a 7 month
business CD beats a liquid business high yield savings by a trivial margin,
Amanda pays a real optionality cost for a nominal gain.

AVERY TO RESOLVE: pull live quotes from 3 institutions, including her existing
bank. Compare 7 month business CD APY against liquid business HYSA and business
money market APY. Compute the actual dollar delta over 7 months on the real
balance, after tax. Recommend proceeding only if the premium is materially
worth the lock.

Ignore headline rates above roughly 6 percent seen in rate roundups. Those are
typically credit union promotional tiers capped at very small balances. Verify
the balance cap before quoting any such rate to Amanda.

### 3.2 The term is a feeling, not a derivation
7 months was chosen intuitively. It should be derived from the credit rebuild
timeline. If Amanda becomes loan ready in month 4 or 5, a month 7 maturity
means being ready and locked at the same time.

AVERY TO RESOLVE: what are the actual credit rebuild milestone dates? Set
maturity at or before loan readiness, not after. If the credit timeline is
itself uncertain, that argues for a no penalty CD or a shorter ladder.

### 3.3 Do not lock the operating float
Kersh Vending is an operating business. Machines fail, product needs restocking,
placement opportunities appear. If all capital is locked and a machine breaks,
Amanda funds the repair personally. That is commingling, it muddies the books,
and it weakens the corporate separation she needs clean for lender review.

AVERY TO RESOLVE: size the operating float from actual transaction history.
What does this business actually spend in a rough month, and what is the
largest single unplanned expense in the record? Float stays liquid. Only true
surplus above the float is CD eligible.

### 3.4 Mechanical traps
- Titling: the CD must be in Kersh Vending Services LLC under the EIN, not
  Amanda's SSN. Miscoded business accounts are painful to unwind.
- Early withdrawal penalties are usually quoted as a number of months of
  interest. Withdraw early enough and the penalty exceeds interest earned and
  reaches into principal. Bounded and small, but not zero. Get the exact
  penalty terms in writing before signing.
- Tax: CD interest is taxable business income. A 7 month CD opened in August
  2026 matures around March 2027 and straddles two tax years. Model it.

## 5. Allocation framework, offered for Avery to adopt or discard

Amanda's own framing: every dollar is a soldier with a job. Proposed tiers for
the Kersh Vending capital. Avery should set the real dollar boundaries from
actual data.

- Tier 0, Operating float. Business checking, fully liquid, never locked.
  Job: keep the machines running without touching personal money.
- Tier 1, Reserve and seasoning. Business savings, liquid, stable balance.
  Job: exist visibly and steadily on the statements a lender will read.
  Seasoned reserves read better on an application than money that just arrived.
- Tier 2, Growth lock. The CD, if it clears section 3.1.
  Job: compound the true surplus and stay out of reach.
- Tier 3, Deal capital. Earmarked down payment.
  Job: be liquid on the date Amanda becomes loan ready, not one day later.

Open tension for Avery to resolve with real lender criteria: Tier 1 and Tier 2
can conflict. A business CD is a visible asset a lender can count, but some
lenders weight liquid reserves differently from locked ones. Find out what her
target lender actually wants to see before deciding how much goes to Tier 2.
