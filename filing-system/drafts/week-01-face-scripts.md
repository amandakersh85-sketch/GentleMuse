> "You've got 5 things broken in your business and you're trying to fix all 5 this week."

**ABSOLVE (3-8s)**
> "That's not a focus problem. You can hold 5 things. You just can't move 5 things at once, and nobody can."

**THE MOVE (8-25s)**
> "Here's what I do on a floor with 4 departments and not enough people. I don't ask what's worst. I ask what's blocking the other 3. There's almost always 1. ## Friday — Order of operations · F-OPERATOR · DECISION
**Sells:** shortcut. The next step, without a call.

**NAME THE MESS (0-3s)**
> "You know what you need to do. You just don't know what to do first."

**ABSOLVE (3-8s)**
> "That's the actual hard part and nobody tells you that. Deciding is the work. The doing is easy once the order exists."

**THE MOVE (8-25s)**
> "When 2 people call off on the same shift, you don't ask what's most urgent, because everything's urgent. You ask what stops the business if nobody touches it, and what's just uncomfortable. **THE COST OF NOT (25-35s)**

**ONE CTA (35-40s)**

**On screen:** `stopped, or just uncomfortable?`
**Caption:** You know what to do, you just don't know what's first. Deciding is the work. Ask what stops the business versus what's only uncomfortable, and half the list moves to next week. Comment DECISION.
