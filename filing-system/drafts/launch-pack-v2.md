# The launch pack, rewritten

Drafted 09/18/2026 during an away session. **Nothing here is scheduled or
posted.** Every caption waits for Amanda.

## Why it needed rewriting

The original pack was written before the positioning existed, and 3 things in
it were broken rather than merely dated. `tests/offer.launchpack.json` keeps
them as a regression case so the gates never stop catching them:

1. **Caption 9 priced the reader's hour in the caption.** "$25-an-hour admin
   between $500-an-hour decisions." That is the house rule with no exceptions:
   a price never appears in a caption, on screen, or in a voiceover. The math
   is still the pitch. It just gets made in hires and tools, not in rates.
2. **The WAITLIST caption pointed at nothing.** WAITLIST is in no automation on
   any account. A reader who commented it got silence. It was also promising
   something new with 3 spots, which is now a real thing: the retainer went
   live today.
3. **The pack sold systems.** The positioning sells an operator. "That is a
   systems problem, and systems can be fixed" is true and it is also what every
   other account on the bench says. What nobody else can say is that a specific
   woman runs real coverage for real people every week.

## The shape every caption holds

From `caption-shape.csv`, all of it measured on her own account: reel not
carousel, about 280 characters, 3 sentences and a short CTA at the end, and 1
line somebody wants to send to a specific person. The pitch never goes in the
body. The magnet is never sold inside the caption.

Each caption below carries 1 live keyword, no price in anything readable, and
nothing that suggests the reader is not capable.

## What Amanda decides

1. **Whether the guarantee may carry a paid CTA.** Caption 7 says "if I can't
   do it, you don't pay me" and then sends the reader to the free check. It
   would be stronger sending them to RETAINER. It does not, because the live
   RETAINER DM says $600 a month on a 3 month minimum and says nothing about
   that guarantee. Putting the 2 together in a caption would create a
   commercial term no buyer has been shown. The fix is to add the term to the
   RETAINER DM first, which needs her approval. Then caption 7 can point at it.
2. **Whether the Walmart floor gets named.** Caption 4 says 4 departments,
   breaks and the truck without naming the employer. Saying it is stronger
   proof and a bigger personal disclosure. Same decision as the face scripts.
3. **Which 10 days these run on.** The dates in the checked queue are
   placeholders, 1 a day. Paid keywords need 120 minutes of clear air from each
   other, which 1 a day clears easily.
4. **CLEANUP appears nowhere in this pack.** It was paused today because
   gentlemuse.co/cleanup answers 404. When that page exists, caption 3 is the
   natural place for it, and it would move the pack's only paid CTA from
   continuity to the one off audit.
