# What 3 to 5 per platform per day actually needs, measured

Written 09/08 after loading until the cap refused. Numbers, not opinion.

## The cap refused at exactly 200

2 of the 3 posts went in. The third came back with "You have reached the
maximum number of scheduled posts (200) for your plan." So the queue is
full, and it is starved at the same time. Both are true and they are not
in conflict.

## Why both are true

The 200 slots are spread across 43 days.

| Window | Days | Posts held | Per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 8 to 19 | 12 | 96 | 8.0 |
| Sep 20 to Oct 31 | 42 | 101 | 2.4 |

A post scheduled for Oct 31 occupies a slot from today until it publishes.
53 days of slot for 1 post. That is what is eating the near days.

**The cap is a window, not a budget.** 200 slots divided by 16 posts a day
(4 platforms at 4) is 12.5 days. If the queue only ever holds the next 12
days and everything past that waits in the reservoir, 200 slots is exactly
the right size for 3 to 5 a day. Held the way it is now, it is 4.6 a day
across 43 days.

## What is actually blocking each thing

In the order that matters, because only one of these is the real one.

**1. Content. This is the binding constraint.**

12 days at 16 a day is 192 posts. At 4 platforms per asset that needs 48
unique reels. The Halloween run has 20 rendered, plus the 5 built today,
plus 3 built and waiting on hosting. 28.

The reservoir's other 93 rows are not near term content. They are next
year's holidays: Christmas 15, Thanksgiving 8, Valentine's 8, St Patrick's
7, and so on. Each needs a plate, and a plate is about 50 credits. 93
plates is roughly 4,650 credits against 2,974 held, so about 59 of the 93
are affordable today and the rest are not.

Every one of the 20 reservoir Halloween facts is already scheduled
somewhere in October. Loading them onto Sep 8 would run the fact weeks
early and duplicate it later, so the reservoir cannot fill the near days
without breaking the season arc.

The SOP already said this: "THE REAL CEILING: 148 unique content ideas,
which caps cadence near 5 posts a day."

**2. Slots. Fixed today.**

The slot model described 3 Instagram slots, 2 TikTok, 2 Facebook and no
YouTube at all, which is why YouTube runs 0 to 1 a day. It now describes
IG 5, TT 4, FB 4, YT 4. Every account's slots are 2 hours or more apart
and none sit in the dead window.

**3. The cap. Not the problem, but it needs the window discipline.**
