## The constraint is not money. It is the same one as last time.

Nov 1 to Jan 1 is 62 days. At 16 posts a day that is **992 posts**.

The 39 holiday facts produce 296 of them. That is **30 percent of the
board**. The other 696 come from lanes that cost nothing to run again:
lead magnet carousels, books, AI and building, home tips, Cesa, Club
Target, food reviews, trivia.

So the money question was never the hard one. Credits buy the seasonal
spine. Volume comes from the evergreen material that is already built and
mostly still unused, which is exactly why hosting the last 4 carousel sets
tonight mattered more than any purchase would have.
