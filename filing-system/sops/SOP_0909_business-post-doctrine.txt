SOP 0909: THE BUSINESS POST DOCTRINE
Adopted 2026-09-09 at Amanda's direction, from the 4C content system she
flagged (competence, credibility, character, consistency). This governs
business-lane content only: the quiz, the Decision Session, the Chaos
Cleanup Intensive, the retainer, and anything pointing at them. It sits on
top of the voice rules in CLAUDE.md and the Cesa separation rule. It never
overrides either.

THE PREMISE
The content is the interview. By the time somebody buys a $47 session or a
$750 intensive, they have already read the posts, scrolled the profile, and
watched how Amanda talks to people. Every business post is doing one of 4
jobs. A draft that does none of them is filler and does not ship.

THE 4 JOBS, IN GENTLE MUSE TERMS

1. COMPETENCE. Show the work.
   Break down 1 bottleneck the way the quiz does: name the specific thing
   that stalls a business and the 1 move that clears it. Practical tips.
   How a real client problem would get solved, step by step.

2. CREDIBILITY. Show receipts, never invent them.
   Real numbers, real before-and-after, real client outcomes, lessons from
   real projects. HARD RULE, from the Sarah incident: no invented clients,
   no composite stories dressed as real ones, no pre-written social proof.
   A hypothetical must be unmistakably hypothetical. If there is no receipt
   yet, post competence instead and say so plainly. Thin and true beats
   specific and invented.

3. CHARACTER. Show what working with her is like.
   How she communicates, how she handles a deadline, a mistake she owned,
   the build-in-public register Just Another Tuesday already carries. This
   is the lane where most sellers lose the client, and it is the lane
   Amanda's voice is strongest in.

4. CONSISTENCY. Stay visible where the buyers are.
   The business buyer looks at LinkedIn and the business lane of the main
   accounts. Target 3 to 5 quality business posts a week, rotating the 4
   jobs. Volume is not the goal; the overall queue already runs 7 to 13
   posts a day. This is about the business lane specifically, which today
   is the thinnest lane in the queue (LinkedIn carries 12 posts of 200).

ROUTING
Every business draft names which of the 4 jobs it does, in 1 word, in the
proposal. post-grader gates it like everything else: 8 of 10 or it does
not ship. The Cesa separation rule applies before anything else: a
business post never touches Cesa content, Cesa accounts, or Cesa threads.
gm_cta_check enforces the account side (P09, H03).
