A DEFECT THIS RUN INTRODUCED AND HAS NOW FIXED

Run 9 taught gm_cta_check.py to refuse a word handed to the reader as a keyword
when that word is in no automation. It was right to. It was also wrong twice.

First, it matched the word "comment" case sensitively. "comment WAITLIST" was
caught and "Comment WAITLIST" was not, and the capitalised form is the one that
starts a sentence, which is where a call to action usually sits. The rule was
half a rule for a day.

Second, and worse, the fix for that could not be applied safely. Making the
match case insensitive would have started refusing BLOOM, BRACELET, BROW,

and blocked the brand deals.

MINDSET COACH, OR PROJECT MANAGER

Amanda: "I don't want to call myself a mindset coach. I prefer to remain project
manager. But technically, I think I'm a mindset coach."

The tables already answer this and they answer project manager. A mindset coach
sells belief change and the client does the work. POS-002 says "I will manage
your project." POS-004 says "if I cannot do it, you do not pay me." Neither is a
promise a coach can make, because a coach is not the one delivering.

POS-003 settles it from the other side. "Not because you are not smart enough,
not because you are not capable." That sentence removes the need for mindset
work rather than selling it. A mindset coach's whole offer is that the reader's
thinking is the problem. Amanda's whole offer is that it is not.

The mindset is how she delivers and what she teaches for free. It is not the
thing she is paid for, and putting it in the title would price her against a
crowded category she would have to out-shout rather than out-deliver. The bench
says the same: CT-022's promise is management systems, not mindset, and she runs
an $85M portfolio on it.

So mindset is the generosity. Management is the offer. That distinction is now
POS-013 and it is the reason the free content has a job.
