A DEFECT THIS RUN INTRODUCED AND HAS NOW FIXED

Run 9 taught gm_cta_check.py to refuse a word handed to the reader as a keyword
when that word is in no automation. It was right to. It was also wrong twice.

First, it matched the word "comment" case sensitively. "comment WAITLIST" was
caught and "Comment WAITLIST" was not, and the capitalised form is the one that
starts a sentence, which is where a call to action usually sits. The rule was
half a rule for a day.

Second, and worse, the fix for that could not be applied safely. Making the
match case insensitive would have started refusing BLOOM, BRACELET, BROW,
BUTTER and 19 more, because the map held Amanda's lead magnets and nothing
else. Those 23 affiliate keywords answer on live published automations. So does
the 13 phrase PR screening set that catches inbound brand enquiries. Every one
of them is a working capture path, and the gate would have called them invented
and blocked the brand deals.

Amanda, 09/18: "nothing wrong with some affiliate stuff, nothing wrong with
brand deals. We can more than happily make branded content. That just improves
our sales skills."

So the map now covers every keyword the platform answers, not the lead magnets
alone. 49 rows, and a Kind column that says what each one is.

  magnet     9   a free thing that captures an email
  offer      4   a paid rung on the ladder
  affiliate  23  a product link Amanda earns on
  screening  13  inbound phrases a brand comments, not a call to action

36 of those 49 were live and invisible to every check in this repo until today.
An affiliate post was being reported as having no capture path at all, because
the only table that knew about keywords had never heard of the keyword it used.
