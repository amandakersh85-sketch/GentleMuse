THE GENTLE MUSE — POSTING CADENCE SOP, 3 TO 5 PER PLATFORM PER DAY

New. Created 09/08/2026. Written after the cadence was misread three times
in a row and a starved queue got thinned instead of filled.

TOOLS: gm_cadence_check.py, gm_queue_plan.py, slot-model.csv,
staging-library.csv
Companions to the Blotato refill loop, not replacements.

WHY THIS SOP EXISTS

posting-cadence.csv used to read "1 reel. Not 2." That line was true once
and stopped being true, and nothing in the data said so. An assistant read
it, believed it, and started thinning a queue that was already running at
1.8 posts a day against a target of 3 to 5.

The correction did not hold the first time either, because the gate written
to enforce it had no column for platform or account. It counted every post
in a day as one number. So a full board of 4 accounts posting 4 times each
came back as "16 posts in a day, over target", and Instagram at 15:00
alongside TikTok at 15:00 came back as a collision. The gate was arguing
for the same wrong answer in a more official voice.

A written rule cannot fix a missing column. This one adds the columns, then
refuses anything that cannot be checked against them.

THE RULE, IN AMANDA'S WORDS
"I want between three to five posts per platform per day."

THE RULE, AS THE GATE ENFORCES IT

  C01  3 to 5 posts per ACCOUNT per day.
       Per account, not per platform. thegentlemuse2026 and
       cesasgoldenyears are 2 audiences and each one gets 3 to 5.
  C02  One post per PLATFORM per timestamp.
       2 platforms at 15:00 is the cross posting model working. It is not
       a collision. The same platform twice at 15:00 is.
  C05  2 hours minimum between posts on ONE ACCOUNT.
       Never measured across accounts. Measured: 2 reels 2 seconds apart
       took 1818 views and 162.
  C06  Nothing between 02:00 and 13:00 UTC.
       That is 21:00 to 08:00 Central. Nobody is there.
       Note 00:00 UTC is 19:00 Central and is prime time, not dead.

THE SLOT MODEL

slot-model.csv is the written answer to "what does a day contain". It ran
for months describing 3 Instagram slots, 2 TikTok, 2 Facebook and no
YouTube row at all, which is the whole reason YouTube posted 0 to 1 times a
day. You cannot load 4 a day against a model that describes 2.

  Instagram 45886   15:00  18:00  20:00  23:00  01:30    (5)
  TikTok    41488   15:00  17:00  20:00  23:00           (4)
  Facebook  30840   14:00  17:00  19:30  22:00           (4)
  YouTube   36129   14:30  17:20  20:20  23:20           (4)

All UTC. Every account's slots are 2 hours or more apart. None sit in the
dead window.

Two of these are load bearing and must not be moved casually:
  - Instagram 15:00 is 10:00 Central, the measured peak at 5528. Moving it
    costs about 25 percent on the one account that cannot afford it.
  - Nothing lands at 19:00 Central. 19:00 Central is 00:00Z the NEXT day,
    and that off by one already cost 4 Cesa posts a day once. The late
    Instagram slot is 20:30 Central for this reason.

THE CAP IS A WINDOW, NOT A BUDGET

Blotato's starter plan holds 200 scheduled posts. That is not a monthly
allowance, it is how many can wait in line at once.

  200 slots / 16 posts a day (4 accounts at 4) = 12.5 days of queue.

So 200 is the right size for 3 to 5 a day, on one condition: the queue
holds a rolling window of about 12 days and everything past that waits in
the reservoir. A post scheduled for Oct 31 owns a slot from the day it is
made until it publishes. Held on 09/08 that is 53 days of slot for 1 post,
and 101 posts held that way is half the queue serving 2.4 posts a day.

On 09/08 the queue was full at 200 and starved at 4.6 a day at the same
time. Both were true. That is what holding far out does.

THE LOOP

1. Before scheduling anything, check what is already there.
   python3 gm_cadence_check.py <queue.csv>
   Input columns: id, postTimeUTC, platform, accountId, label
   Without platform and accountId it checks as a single lane, which is
   right for 1 account and wrong for a board.

2. Compare the near window to the model.
   python3 gm_queue_plan.py --queue live.json --start <today> --days 14

3. Fill the holes oldest need first, from staging-library.csv STAGED rows.
   Before each one, confirm three things:
     - the FACT is not already scheduled on another day. Running a fact
       early and again later is a duplicate, not volume.
     - the MEDIA is not already worn by a scheduled post. Recycled
       captions never re-wear media. Carousel campaigns are the exception.
     - the proposed day still passes the gate WITH the new post in it.
       Build the day, run the gate, then schedule. Not after.

4. Load until the cap refuses. It refuses with "You have reached the
   maximum number of scheduled posts (200) for your plan." That message is
   the expected end of a run, not a failure.

5. Record every scheduled post in blitz-schedule.csv with its
   SubmissionId, and every refusal as Status cap-blocked, so the next pass
   knows where it stopped.

WHAT TO DO WHEN THE QUEUE IS FULL AND STARVED

In this order, because only one of these is usually the real one.

  1. Is it CONTENT? 12 days at 16 a day needs 192 posts, which is 48
     unique reels at 4 platforms each. Count what is rendered. On 09/08
     it was 28. That is the answer most of the time, and no amount of
     scheduling fixes it.
  2. Is it SLOTS? Check slot-model.csv actually describes the cadence you
     are trying to run.
  3. Is it the WINDOW? Look at how far out the queue reaches. If the tail
     is thin and long, it is eating the near days.

Never propose a plan upgrade. Amanda has ruled on that twice. Extra
credits bought separately are fine.

THE REAL CEILING

The refill SOP already said it and it is still true: about 148 unique
content ideas exist, which caps sustainable cadence near 5 posts a day per
account. Cadence beyond that is not a scheduling problem.

TESTS
bash filing-system/tests/run-tests.sh
The cadence cases are named. A full 4 platform board must read as clean,
2 platforms at the same minute must not be a collision, and 2 accounts on
one platform must be counted apart. If those 3 fail, the gate has drifted
back to counting a day as one number and it will start arguing for
thinning again.
