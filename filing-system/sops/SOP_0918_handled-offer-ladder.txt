WHY THIS RUN EXISTS

The pivot to HANDLED arrived as 3 prose handoffs. Each one carried its own copy
of the prices, the keywords and the offer names, and each one called itself the
source of truth. The handoff dated 09/17 closes with the rule that holds the
whole arrangement together:

  "When one of us changes a standing fact (price, keyword, URL), the others
  update every downstream artifact in the same turn. No stale terms
  resurfacing."

That rule was already broken at the moment it was written. The automations were
read live and compared against it. 5 of its standing facts were wrong:

  It says DECISION does not exist in Blotato and must never be used as a call
  to action. Automation 451 answers DECISION on the main Instagram account, is
  active, is published, and sells The Decision Map at $47.

  It says FALLFIT does not exist. Automation 2277 answers it.

  It says CESA is blocked by a validation bug and that "comment CESA" must
  never be written. Automations 445, 432 and 2952 all answer CESA, all 3 are
  active and published. The real CESA failure is in the keyword audit and it is
  a different failure: 2 of 4 runs returned "the comment you are trying to reply
  to already has a reply", which is what happens when one comment is used twice.

  It says PRINCESS replaces CESA. PRINCESS answers on 1 account, the senior dog
  account, as 1 of 47 trigger phrases on automation 2952. It has never answered
  on the main Instagram account or on Facebook. Telling a main account post to
  say PRINCESS sends it to an automation that is not listening.

  It says GUIDE is Instagram only and CONSIDER is the senior dog account only.
  GUIDE answers on both main accounts. CONSIDER answers on all 3.

It was right about WAITLIST. WAITLIST is in no automation, and the launch pack
ships a post telling readers to comment it.

The prices drifted the same way. The handoff proposes HANDLED Monthly at $750 a
month. $750 is already the price of The Chaos Cleanup Plan, a different product,
sitting in a drafted automation. The retainer that HANDLED Monthly repackages is
drafted at $600 a month on a 3 month minimum. One number had come to mean 2
things, and nothing in the chain could see it.


WHY THIS IS A TABLE AND NOT A REMINDER

Run 6 established the pattern. When the same failure lands twice, the fix is the
missing field and a check that refuses to ship without it, because guidance
governs judgment and judgment is what already failed.

"Update every downstream artifact in the same turn" is guidance. It asks 3
assistants to each remember the same 30 facts and to notice when one changes. It
failed inside its own document.

So the standing facts now live in 2 tables that are compared against the
platform by a script, and the prose handoffs become what they should have been
from the start, which is commentary.


THE MISSING FIELD

PriceStatus. It is not anybody's memory of whether Amanda approved a price. It
is evidenced by where the price actually lives, which is checkable:

  live        a customer can see this price right now, on a published surface
  drafted     written into an automation that exists and is not published
  proposed    exists only in a document
  unverified  named in a document and found nowhere at all

The handoffs say "never publish a price she has not approved" 3 times across 2
files. That sentence has no machine meaning. PriceStatus does. Only live may
appear on a customer facing surface, and live means it is already published,
which is the only form of approval that leaves a trace.

HOW TO RUN IT

  python3 scripts/gm_offer_check.py --ladder
  python3 scripts/gm_offer_check.py --queue queue.json
  python3 scripts/gm_offer_check.py --sync automations.json
  python3 scripts/gm_offer_check.py --sync automations.json --scope BOTTLENECK

The sync export is whatever the Blotato automations listing returns, saved as
JSON. Run it before any batch that names a price or a keyword, and after any
change made in Blotato. It reports 5 kinds of disagreement between the tables
and the platform: a keyword the map calls live that nothing answers, a keyword
answering that the map calls draft, an account set that differs, a price in a
DM that differs from the ladder, and a destination URL that differs from the map.

Affiliate and PR automations are deliberately outside the magnet map. The sync
lists them under their own heading and does not count them as findings. There
are 38 of them.

Exit 0 pass, 1 fail, 2 hold. Regression suite: bash filing-system/tests/run-tests.sh.

THE NEW FIELD: CreditStatus
Adding CreditsToward to a CSV does not create a credit. It creates a term of
sale that no customer has been told, which is the same shape of failure as a
keyword that is live in a document and dead on the platform. Run 9 built
PriceStatus for exactly that reason and it proved itself this morning when $750
went live and the price gate changed behaviour with no rule edited.

So a credit now carries the same kind of field:

  live      the buyer is told, on a published surface
  drafted   written into something unpublished
  proposed  exists only in this table
  none      the rung credits toward nothing

H02_CREDIT_UNPUBLISHED holds a priced, real rung that names a credit at
anything other than live. It fires once today, on OF-004: the ladder had the
Plan crediting toward the Monthly Retainer, and it does not. The RETAINER DM
recommends starting with the Plan, which is a recommendation and not a credit,
and reading one as the other is how a term nobody promised ends up in a table.
That is now recorded honestly and held for Amanda rather than quietly asserted.
