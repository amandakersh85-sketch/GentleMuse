SOP 09/09/2026 — THE TRIVIA PIPELINE (Run 8)
The Gentle Muse. Supersedes nothing. Additive to the posting board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. WHAT THIS LANE IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Amanda's call, 09/08/2026: the trivia runs on Gentle Muse, on the 4
platforms already connected, and it is scoped to AI, automation and the
creator economy. Not general trivia.

The reason is not taste. General trivia fills the same slots at the same
cost while diluting the positioning the account exists to carry. Scoped
trivia fills those slots AND reinforces it. The scope is enforced in data,
in the Topic column, so it cannot drift at 1am:

    ai | automation | creator

Anything else is refused by the bank, not argued about.

Why the lane exists at all: the board needs distinct facts. On 09/08 the
video pool held 17 Halloween facts against 132 video slots in 11 days, and
that shortage is what produced both the repeats and the gaps. A daily
trivia cut is 4 genuinely new posts a day across YouTube, TikTok, Facebook
and Instagram. It is the only lever on the table that adds material without
a credit spend.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. THE RISK THIS PIPELINE IS BUILT AROUND
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Run 6 stopped a caption being bound to a clip that did not show it.
Run 7 stopped a caption being bound to a holiday fact nobody checked.
Run 8 is the same guard on the lane where it matters most.

The audience for AI and creator trivia is an audience that will know when a
number is wrong. The account's whole proposition is being the one that gets
it right. A confident invented figure, in her voice, costs more than a week
of silence.

Two failures this lane has that the holiday lane does not:

  A newsletter is not a source.  A newsletter is where a fact was FOUND.
  What makes it true is the thing itself: the company's own post, the
  paper, the filing, the documentation. A summary of a summary is how a
  wrong number ends up being said out loud. FoundIn and Source are 2
  different columns and the gate refuses a row where Source is just the
  newsletter again.

  Facts move.  Nobody is going to change what year Goosebumps started. A
  price, a model name, a user count or a policy changes constantly. A fact
  true in March and repeated in September is not a fact, it is a mistake.
  Rows marked Decays are held once VerifiedOn is more than 90 days old.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. THE PIPELINE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1  HUNT       Read the newsletters. Pull candidates only. Nothing that
                comes out of this stage is a fact yet, it is a lead.

  2  VERIFY     Open the primary source. Not the newsletter's link text,
                the actual thing. If it cannot be opened and read, the
                candidate is dropped. Not softened, dropped.

  3  BANK       Write the row into data/trivia-fact-bank.csv. Fact,
                Backbone, FoundIn, Source, SourceUrl, AsOf, Decays.
                Verified stays "no" until a human has read the source.

  4  GATE       python3 scripts/gm_trivia_bank.py --audit
                Every held row prints the reason it is held. Fix the row
                or leave it held. Never substitute a nearby fact.

  5  WRITE      One script per fact, then per-platform captions. The
                Backbone has to survive into the caption. A fact reported
                without a turn is trivia, and trivia is not the product.

  6  CHECK      python3 scripts/gm_trivia_check.py --post <caption.json>
                Exit 0 PASS, 1 FAIL, 2 HOLD. Then post-grader, 8+/10.

  7  APPROVE    Amanda. Every time. See section 5.

When the n8n workflow runs steps 5 and 6, a fact enters it through
gm_trivia_trigger.py and nowhere else. Section 8.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. THE GATE, RULE BY RULE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Bank rules (gm_trivia_bank.py)

  no Fact line              under 20 characters is not a claim
  no Backbone               under 15 characters is a fact with no turn
  Topic outside the lane    ai, automation, creator, nothing else
  no Source                 blank, "n/a", "tbd" and friends
  Source is a newsletter    provenance mistaken for authority
  Source equals FoundIn     nothing was checked, it was copied
  no SourceUrl              nobody can check it in 1 click
  not Verified              a human has not read the source yet
  Decays and stale          past the 90 day window, re-check before use

Caption rules (gm_trivia_check.py)

  T00_NO_FACT_ID            HOLD. No fact named, so nothing to check
                            against. Write the fact down first.
  T01_UNKNOWN_FACT          FAIL. A fact not in the bank was not checked.
  T02_FACT_NOT_USABLE       FAIL. Relays the bank's reasons.
  T03_NUMBER_NOT_IN_BANK    FAIL. Every number the caption asserts must
                            appear in the bank row. THIS IS THE ONE THAT
                            DOES THE WORK. Asked to write 40 seconds
                            about a 2017 paper, a model will happily add
                            a citation count, a funding round or a market
                            size nobody asked for and nobody checked.
                            Those extra numbers are the entire risk and
                            they are mechanically findable.
  T04_NO_TURN               FAIL. The Backbone did not survive into the
                            caption.
  T05_OFF_LANE              FAIL. Topic outside the scope.
  T06_VOICE                 FAIL. Em dash.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. APPROVAL, AND WHY IT IS NOT AUTOMATED
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

House rule 4: approval is the gate. Machine verdicts are proposals.

A PASS from either script means "nothing here is provably wrong." It does
not mean "this is true." Only a person who opened the source knows that.
The pipeline produces a HOLD queue. Amanda releases it.

This is not caution for its own sake. Unattended publishing on this lane
buys 4 posts a day and risks the reason the account is followed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. COST
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Per fact, delivered to 4 platforms:

  verification        time only
  script + captions   time only
  voiceover           about $0.02 per line at the measured HeyGen rate
  talking head        HeyGen, using Amanda's own avatar twin. It already
                      exists, 2 groups of it. Listing avatars without
                      --ownership private returns only stock and makes it
                      look like it does not.
  render              local, free
  distribution        1 Blotato slot per platform, 4 of the 200

At 1 fact a day that is roughly $0.10 a week of HeyGen wallet and 28 of
the 200 scheduled slots. It is the cheapest material on the board.

The binding constraint is not money, it is the 200 slot cap. 4 more posts
a day shortens the runway. See C12_RUNWAY_END in gm_cadence_check.py, and
fill the nearest empty days first.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. THE BANK SHIPS EMPTY ON PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

trivia-fact-bank.csv ships with 3 rows and 0 of them usable. Every one is
marked Verified=no.

They are seeded so the shape is obvious and the gate is demonstrably
working, and they are unverified because nobody has opened the sources
yet. Marking them verified to make the pipeline look ready is exactly the
failure this whole run exists to prevent.

First production job: open the 3 URLs, read them, and set Verified=yes and
VerifiedOn only for the ones that hold up.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. THE N8N TRIGGER (added 09/28/2026)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The lane is being automated in n8n: a webhook, then OpenAI for the script,
HeyGen for the render, Blotato for the schedule. The webhook design that
arrived on 09/28 broke 4 rules this SOP already states.

  It took a topic.     The example was "World History - Forgotten
                       Inventions". That is outside the lane, and a topic
                       handed to a model asks the model to supply the
                       facts. Section 2 is why that cannot happen.
  It had no key.       The setup table recommended Header Auth and the
                       node JSON left it off. Run on n8n 2.40.7, the node
                       as given answered a call with no key at all, HTTP
                       200, and started the workflow. That is a HeyGen
                       spend and a post in her name for whoever finds the
                       URL.
  It answered first.   With the default Respond setting the caller hears
                       "Workflow was started" before anything has looked
                       at what was sent, so nothing can refuse it.
  It ended at Blotato. Section 5. The render goes to HOLD. Amanda
                       releases it.

It also named 2 platforms and a placeholder voice. The lane is 4
platforms, and the voice is hers.

What was built instead

  n8n/daily-trivia-trigger.json   the workflow to import. A webhook, POST,
                                  path daily-trivia-trigger, Header Auth
                                  on. Then the check, then the answer to
                                  the caller, then a gate that lets only
                                  an accepted fact go on.
  n8n/trivia-payload-check.js     the check. Anything that is not 1
                                  checked fact for the 4 platforms in her
                                  voice gets HTTP 422 and every reason.
  scripts/gm_trivia_trigger.py    the sender. Picks the fact, builds the
                                  payload, proposes, sends under --send.

The workflow file is built, not written. gm_trivia_trigger.py --workflow
--write reads the lane from gm_trivia_bank.py and the platforms from
channel-rules.csv. A rule changed there reaches the webhook once the file
is rebuilt and imported again, and the test suite fails for as long as the
committed file is out of date with the rules.

The payload

  fact_id, topic, fact, backbone,    the bank row, only when the bank says
  source, source_url, as_of,         it can carry a post on the day it airs
  verified_on, delivery
  publish_date                       the day, YYYY-MM-DD. Not a time. Each
                                     platform's time comes from its slot
                                     when it is scheduled
  target_platform, accounts          channel-rules.csv: the channels that
                                     carry everything, main accounts only
  cta                                per platform. The keyword where an
                                     automation answers it, the link where
                                     a link is clickable. TikTok gets
                                     neither, its ask is the bio
  voice_id, voice_speed, avatar_id   her voice at 0.92, 1 of the 2 podcast
                                     looks, rotated by day

Setting it up, once

  1  In n8n, import filing-system/n8n/daily-trivia-trigger.json.
  2  On the webhook node, Credential for Header Auth, create new. Name
     X-Trivia-Key. Value a long random string. The value never goes in
     this repo.
  3  Publish the workflow, or switch it Active in older n8n. Copy the
     production URL, the one with /webhook/ in it, not /webhook-test/.
  4  Where the sender runs, set N8N_TRIVIA_URL to that URL and
     N8N_TRIVIA_KEY to the value from step 2.

Until step 2 is done the webhook refuses every call with HTTP 500, "No
authentication data defined on node!". That is the safe way round.

Sending

  python3 scripts/gm_trivia_trigger.py --next           proposes
  python3 scripts/gm_trivia_trigger.py --next --send    sends
  python3 scripts/gm_trivia_trigger.py --fact TRV-004   a named fact

--next takes the bank's pull order, passes over facts already sent and
motion-text facts, and names each one it passes. It stops at a fact it
cannot send rather than skipping to one it can.

Every accepted send is written to data/trivia-trigger-log.csv. Commit that
line. It is what stops a fact rendering twice, and a second render of a
fact that already has one is regenerating what exists. --resend is only
for a run that failed after it was accepted.

Exit 0 proposed or accepted, 1 refused, 2 nothing sent or nothing
confirmed.

Built, and not built, on 09/28

Built and run on n8n 2.40.7: the webhook, the key, the check, the answer
and the gate. An accepted fact reaches a placeholder node, "Next: script
from the fact". A refused one stops before it.

Not built: the OpenAI, HeyGen, HOLD and Blotato nodes. Whoever builds them
holds to 4 things.

  The script is written from payload.fact and payload.backbone and nothing
  else. The pasted {{ $json.body.topic }} now carries only the lane word.
  Every number in the script has to be in the fact, which is T03. n8n
  cannot run gm_trivia_check.py itself, so how the captions reach that
  check is the first thing to settle, before any HeyGen node exists.

  HeyGen renders payload.avatar_id speaking with payload.voice_id at
  payload.voice_speed.

  The workflow stops at HOLD. Amanda approves the render and the captions
  before any Blotato node runs.

  Blotato posts to payload.accounts with payload.cta per platform, at each
  platform's own slot, inside the 200 slot cap, and through the cadence
  gates in HANDOFF_0909 section 7.

Not turned on: any daily schedule. It comes after the nodes past the gate
exist. The trigger only ever sends a verified fact, so the bank sets the
pace: the 3 seeded rows were read against their sources on 09/28 (PR #27),
and each fact sent is logged and not sent again, so the hunt in section 3
has to keep adding verified rows.
