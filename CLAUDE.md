# Gentle Muse — Claude Working Rules

## HARD RULE: Product links in captions (locked in 08/21/2026, from Amanda directly)

If a video names or shows a specific product, the caption MUST carry the link
to THAT product. Not the storefront. Not "link in bio." The product's own link.

For Target / Club Target content:
- Specific product link format: https://club.target.com/s/amanda.20/_/sku/{SKU}
- Storefront (only when the video is about the whole storefront, never as a
  substitute for a specific product): https://club.target.com/a/amanda.20
- Required disclosure on every Club Target post: "I'm a Target partner, so I
  may earn rewards or commission when you shop my link, at no extra cost to
  you. #ad" plus #TargetPartner and the active challenge hashtag.

For other affiliate content: use the platform tracked links (gm-tiktok-20,
gm-insta-20, gm-web-20, gm-pin-20). DMs route to the website link, never a raw
Amazon link. Never state a price on affiliate content.

Before scheduling ANY product video: confirm the SKU or product link exists in
the caption. If the SKU is unknown, ask Amanda or look it up. Do not ship the
post without it.

Known miss for the record: the FlavCity protein smoothie TikTok (published
08/20/2026, #TargetEverydayEssentials) named the product but carried only the
storefront link. The heyday phone case TikTok from the same day did it right
(sku 94705110).

No em dashes. Digits, not spelled-out numbers. Contractions always. Warm,
grounded, practical, no hype. Instagram max 5 hashtags. Facebook and LinkedIn
